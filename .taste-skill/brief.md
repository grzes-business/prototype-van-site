# Wendrowan: brief, inventory, argument, idea (design-taste-frontend steps 1, 2, 4)

Direction: page-is-the-object (seed 1675685273). Palette: Forest and lime. Type redraw: Big Shoulders Display / Hanken Grotesk, self-hosted (Switzer is not on npm).

Inventory: wędrować + van; one van only; two pickup places; no kilometre limit; pop-top roof; 4 berths 2×2; 5 seats; 12V + 220V; 3×3 m awning tent; high season 1 May to 30 Sep; 200 zł service fee; 4000 zł deposit; destination left to the renter.

Argument
- Hero: you decide where this goes. Proof: the blank destination on the ticket.
- Van: everything for the trip is on board. Proof: full equipment list, optional extras added to your ticket.
- Pricing: no surprises. Proof: every fee, no km limit.
- Booking: check and request in one go. Proof: calendar from Google, live total.
- Questions: last doubts. Proof: plain answers and direct contact.

Idea: On this page, the one thing you are renting (one van, your dates, a destination nobody else chooses) becomes a printed ticket with DO left blank.
Competitor test: a rental with a fleet sells a car class. This sells one seat-less ticket for one van, with pickup stations printed and the destination yours.
Live thing: type a destination, the split-flap board shows it, and it stays on your ticket through extras, dates and total until you send it and the stub tears off.
